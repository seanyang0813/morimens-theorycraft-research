# General card-resource sequence

`engine/card-resource-timeline.mjs` and `node tools/run_card_resource_timeline.mjs timeline.json` project energy through normal non-keeper PvE card checks and payment. This is character-independent; Mouchette is not encoded into the model.

Schema version 1 requires kind `morimens-card-resource-timeline`, explicit PC build, interveningEffects `assumed-absent`, initialEnergy, and steps. Each step has a unique id and cardInstanceId, costInput without energy, and explicit conditions accepted by the card-resource resolver. The previous step is the sole source of available energy. Invalid inputs anywhere reject before execution; a failed play check stops the projection without payment. Its attempted trace and all earlier payments remain inspectable. Reusing a card instance is refused because draws, retention and return-to-hand effects are not reconstructed.

The composition demonstrates a fixed-cost-3 card followed by X completing from 5 energy, while X then that fixed card stops at the energy check. These are synthetic resource examples, not real build predictions. The sequence does not execute damage, refund/listener effects, cost changes, turn gates or hand mutations; per-card state and hand membership are explicitly supplied. Component runtime evidence does not establish a complete original sequence. The separate [card action timeline](CARD_ACTION_TIMELINE.md) now connects resource checks and payment to supplied hits and optional controlled Old Embers effects under shared energy, HP, shield and layer state.
