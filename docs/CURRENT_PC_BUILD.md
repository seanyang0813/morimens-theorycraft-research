# Current PC combat-build boundary

The installed Steam client now uses downloaded resource 150 / content build 51. The calculator and its broad original-runtime suite remain pinned to resource 144 / build 51. `research/evidence/pc-res144-to-res150-combat-build.json` compares hash-only TextAsset inventories from the two checkpoints and records the installed download manifest and Steam build without account or replay identifiers.

Of the selected combat modules, BattleConst, BattleUtilServer, BattleCmdServer, BattlePropertyServer, BEActiveDamage, BattleRecord and BattleEngine changed. BattleUnitBase, BattleStateServer, BEPureDamage, BEFixedDamage, BETentacleAttack, BattleReplayPlayer, PVEGameplay and BattleUnitUtil remained byte-identical. Path IDs are ignored because bundle repacking changes them without changing content.

The actual resource-150 chunks were loaded through the copied client XLua runtime. `tools/verify_current_show_damage_formula.py` evaluated all 2,262 existing ShowDamageFormula fixture vectors and matched resource 144 exactly. `tools/verify_current_target_damage.py` then executed the changed BattleCmdServer with the resource-150 constants and utility module; all 2,097 existing no-card `__GetFinalDamage` and `GetTargetBeDmgPerMul` fixtures also matched exactly. The source hashes and results are in `research/evidence/pc-res150-show-damage-runtime.json` and `research/evidence/pc-res150-target-damage-runtime.json`.

The selected changed property paths also remain equal: 24 numeric constructor/mastery-initialization cases and 16 ordinary HP-subtraction cases match exactly. The changed BattleEngine also reproduces all 24 inherited ordinary PvE command-entry gate cases, through the missing-card dispatch boundary. Active effect initialization does contain a confirmed change. In resource 144, repeat modifiers are applied to the supplied repeat and the result is then clamped to at least one. Resource 150 first clamps the base repeat to at least one, then applies the flat and percentage repeat modifiers, and finally clamps again. This changes 12 of the 113 inherited initialization probes, all with supplied repeat -1 or 0; 8 live per-target binding probes remain equal. `engine/active-damage-command.mjs` now preserves both orders behind an explicit build identifier.

This establishes cross-build equality for the utility formula, inherited no-card final target/critical domain, selected property paths and the narrow command-entry gate, while recording the Active repeat-order change explicitly. It does not cover card/tag critical branches excluded by the old target suite, offensive-input assembly, broader property mutation, full Active effect routing, recording or engine scheduling after dispatch. A resource-150 replay cannot validate the resource-144 end-to-end calculator until every exercised changed method is separately reviewed and runtime-tested, or the calculator is ported to an independently tested resource-150 runtime.

For a future current-build holdout, capture the resource-150 manifest and script-bundle hashes before the battle, freeze the prediction before outcome inspection, and bind the replay to the same live session. Historical replay timestamps or embedded data rows alone do not establish its engine code.
