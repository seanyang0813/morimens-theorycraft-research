# Current PC combat-build boundary

The installed Steam client now uses downloaded resource 150 / content build 51. The calculator and its broad original-runtime suite remain pinned to resource 144 / build 51. `research/evidence/pc-res144-to-res150-combat-build.json` compares hash-only TextAsset inventories from the two checkpoints and records the installed download manifest and Steam build without account or replay identifiers.

Of the selected combat modules, BattleConst, BattleUtilServer, BattleCmdServer, BattlePropertyServer, BEActiveDamage, BattleRecord and BattleEngine changed. BattleUnitBase, BattleStateServer, BEPureDamage, BEFixedDamage, BETentacleAttack, BattleReplayPlayer, PVEGameplay and BattleUnitUtil remained byte-identical. Path IDs are ignored because bundle repacking changes them without changing content.

The actual resource-150 chunks were loaded through the copied client XLua runtime. `tools/verify_current_show_damage_formula.py` evaluated all 2,262 existing ShowDamageFormula fixture vectors and matched resource 144 exactly. The changed BattleCmdServer also matches 879 inherited PvE offensive-input assembly cases: 549 without a card and 330 with an explicit non-Awake card. `tools/verify_current_target_damage.py` checks another 2,097 no-card `__GetFinalDamage` and `GetTargetBeDmgPerMul` cases. The card extension matches 506 cases covering none/Strike/Ulti/combined tags, card and tag critical contributions, Ulti and instruction-card target amplification, state-trigger exclusion and supplied block/barrier eligibility. The source hashes and results are in the corresponding `pc-res150-*-runtime.json` evidence files.

The selected changed property paths also remain equal: 24 numeric constructor/mastery-initialization cases and 16 ordinary HP-subtraction cases match exactly. The combined BeHit path matches another 18 cases covering explicit immunity, block, puncture, retained-HP, incoming-limit and death-resistance inputs through HP mutation. The changed BattleEngine reproduces all 24 inherited ordinary PvE command-entry gate cases through the missing-card dispatch boundary. Fourteen selected `BattleRecord` cases also match: ten frame constructors for card use, hits, property changes, target selection and state changes, plus four queue lifecycles through battle-cut dispatch. Seven `RunEffectOrder` scenarios match across fresh/resumed orders, post-action refresh, PvE/PvP statistics, cleanup, battle/wave completion and forced-bout routing. Six `lg_UseCard` scenarios match for missing/denied/successful cards, server-target clearing, client-target preservation/replacement and the per-turn play limit. A wider card-path hash check finds BattleUnitBase, BattleCardMgrServer, BattleCardServer, BEBeforeUseCard and BEAfterUseCard unchanged; BattleUnitPlayer changed. Its direct and connected energy-payment paths nevertheless match all 222 inherited cases, including X-cost and ignore/force-mode branches through property mutation. The byte-identical `BattleUnitBase.CanUseCard` also matches all 90 inherited ordinary non-keeper PvE eligibility cases when executed with resource-150 dependencies. Eight ordinary `UseCard`/effect-chain construction cases match for missing and hard-blocked cards, target generation or explicit targets, optional pre-commands, and the `RunEffectOrder` wrapper. Eight after-use cases match explicit/Awake/consumed/attached/graveyard routing, used-card statistics, normal and attached event requests, and end-of-action cleanup. Active effect initialization does contain a confirmed change. In resource 144, repeat modifiers are applied to the supplied repeat and the result is then clamped to at least one. Resource 150 first clamps the base repeat to at least one, then applies the flat and percentage repeat modifiers, and finally clamps again. This changes 12 of the 113 inherited initialization probes, all with supplied repeat -1 or 0; 8 live per-target binding probes and 16 repetition-routing probes remain equal. The routing probes cover owner termination, per-repeat delay, dead single-target regeneration and child-effect creation order. `engine/active-damage-command.mjs` now preserves both initialization orders and exposes the tested routing transition.

This establishes cross-build equality for the utility formula, inherited ordinary PvE offensive assembly, no-card and Strike/Ulti card final target/critical domains, selected property paths, Active repetition routing, the narrow command-entry gate and connected event delivery, while recording the Active repeat-order change explicitly. BattleEffectServer, BattleEffectMgrServer, BEFunctionEffect, BEAttachPostAction, BESendEvent, BattleLogicEvent, BattleEventMgr, Table and BERoleDeadlyDamage are byte-identical, carrying their existing effect/event/fatal-damage evidence forward to resource 150. `BERoleDie` and `BattleUnitMonster` changed; the new path delegates PvE and first-free-respawn eligibility to role methods. Executing the actual changed `BERoleDie`, `BattleUnitMonster` and `BattleUnitPlayer` chunks with resource-150 constants matches all eight selected missing-role, revived, monster, PvE-player, PvP and monster-eligibility fixtures. The main skill-phase boundary is narrower than the earlier inventory implied: BEGenerateTargets, BECreateSkillPhase, BattleCmdTargetsExp, BattleEffectServer and BattleEffectMgrServer are byte-identical. Within the changed BattleCmdServer, BattleCmdParser, BattlePropertyServer and BEActiveDamage path, 257 inherited cases now match exactly across connected phase start through `TriggerCmd`, original command-to-effect-manager construction, real Active and Function-effect construction/target and parameter binding/initialization/repetition routing/function dispatch into `Damage2SingleTarget`, `GetRealDmg`, byte-identical `BattleUnitBase.BeHit`, HP mutation, original nonlethal event-request order and one lethal monster request boundary, phase cleanup, StateOwner target handoff, ArgN lookup, connected skill-argument construction, command-delay planning and effect-row ordering. The lethal case reaches zero HP, requests `BERoleDeadlyDamage` then `BERoleDie`, and requests `ActiveDamageKill` after `DoDamage` and `BeDamage`. The connected hit cases use explicit neutral PvE adapters and empty skill types; property-send, hit-record and event-factory callbacks are observers. Event delivery is proven separately, but those specific damage listeners and any effects they generate are not yet connected to the hit path; animation and a fully scheduled death tree also remain outside the boundary. A separate live-catalog audit finds 69 Awake skills and no directly linked damage rows in either build; one-level literal traversal reaches one conditional Passive-damage row, and resource 150 adds one direct RemoveState row. Awake nested/state/resource execution, broader property mutation, keeper/PvP/forced-play eligibility outside the selected cases, listener-generated effect trees, full death scheduling, card-manager mutation internals, transport serialization/replay playback and other untested component internals remain uncovered. A resource-150 replay cannot validate the resource-144 end-to-end calculator until every exercised changed method is separately reviewed and runtime-tested, or the calculator is ported to an independently tested resource-150 runtime.

For a future current-build holdout, capture the resource-150 manifest and script-bundle hashes before the battle, freeze the prediction before outcome inspection, and bind the replay to the same live session. Historical replay timestamps or embedded data rows alone do not establish its engine code.
