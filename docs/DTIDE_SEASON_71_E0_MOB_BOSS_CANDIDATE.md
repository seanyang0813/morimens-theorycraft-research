# Season 71: investment-aware mob-and-boss candidate

**Proposed Zone 5 team for testing:** Mouchette / Helot / Ramona / Doll. The E0-only card calculation below is a *rule floor*, not a realistic proposed build for the highest difficulty. A breakpoint-based comparison case is **Mouchette E3 / Helot E1 / Ramona E0 / Doll E2**, with actual levels, skills and Wheels supplied from the roster before any damage prediction. This is a source-derived team concept, **not a tier-7 clear prediction** or a claim that the user's roster contains these builds. Two invested SSRs can still be more expensive than one heavily invested SSR; compare actual costs and owned progression rather than rarity counts.

The breakpoint case is chosen for explicit unlocked effects, not an invented minimum investment: installed `AwakerPotency` rows 122471/122474 give Mouchette's E2 Mortal Blast copy and E3 Exalt extra damage instance; row 13538 gives Helot's E1 opening Irregular Form; Doll rows 13533/13060 give E1 Surgery healing improvement and E2 Equal Exchange healing/shield improvement. Ramona's basic retrieval exists at E0. These upgrades improve card flow or survival, but **none proves a mob kill**. The observed level-90, potency-15 Mouchette example below is a warning that raising Enlighten alone does not establish enough area damage.

At this breakpoint, the order of effects matters. `Skill` 122486 and `Cmd` 122491 show that Mouchette's **first ordinary E3 Exalt** makes one all-enemy hit with level-one raw coefficient `2.50 × her BattleAtkForce`, **then** adds state 124039. A later Mortal Blast reads that state, giving three all-enemy hits of `0.15 × her BattleAtkForce`, or `0.45 × her BattleAtkForce` per enemy. The two actions together are therefore a **conditional `2.95 × Mouchette BattleAtkForce` per enemy** before crit, defense, other modifiers and any unrelated follow-up. This is not a same-turn guarantee: Exalt needs 100 Aliemus, Rouse and Blast must be accessible, and energy/costs must permit the sequence. Subsequent Exalts can differ because 124039 already exists. The source-checked script now prints this breakpoint branch separately from its E0 floor.

The transferable idea from [an older budget D-Tide report](https://www.reddit.com/r/Morimens/comments/1ukvo00/425_dtide_budget_team_no_lv80_oe_limit/) is to pair Mouchette with Helot for card flow. That player's historical result does not establish a Season 71 clear. Here the specific Season 71 adaptation is to use **Jade Imprint on Helot's original Long Hate** when it is eligible in hand after Helot's Rouse, creating one Exhaust copy each in hand, draw and discard. The current Zone 5 SS replay started with Jade Imprint and Chaos Ring; tier-7 starting relics remain unconfirmed directly. Jade's rule requires a card belonging to the Roused Awakener; Rousing Mouchette cannot select Helot's card.

## Source-derived roles and action order

1. **Mobs:** Helot's Long Hate (`Skill` 4010) costs 1, hits `AllEnemy`, loses 10% of current team HP, applies Bleeding equal to its damage, and creates two random Awakener Strike cards with Exhaust. It is present at E0. Mouchette's Rouse (`Skill` 122485) generates Mortal Blast at E0; Mortal Blast (`Skill` 122483) costs 1 and hits `AllEnemy` twice. Generated Mortal Blast itself is not a legal Jade target because Jade requires a non-derived card.
2. **Boss:** Use the generated Strikes and Mouchette's own Strike cards to build her Strike-trigger and Storm Strike/Mortal Blast line. Helot's generated Strikes feed card flow, but their owners and targeting are random; no specific draw or damage is promised. The Sage's first depleted bar refills at double maximum HP, demonstrated in a current-season SS replay, so reserve damage and healing for the second bar.
3. **Resource and survival:** Ramona can retrieve a Jade copy from draw/discard when her retrieval is available, but the Sage's first Posse each turn permanently raises Posse cost. Doll's ordinary E0 Surgery (`Skill` 4365) costs 2 and heals while applying Weakness to all enemies. Helot's HP spending makes this slot purposeful; Doll's actual healing is not yet quantified against tier-7 attacks. If survival fails, test Ogier instead of Ramona, giving up the planned immediate discard-copy retrieval.

The team has **three Chaos Awakeners and one non-Chaos Awakener** (`AwakerConfig` 94450, 15595, 15570 and 15587). Under Chaos Ring, Helot does not receive a Chaos Dimensional Image and contributes no Chaos-member creation-cap increase. This is the measurable cost of including his E0 area card; do not model a four-Chaos bonus.

## Bounded calculation

Run `node tools/season71_mob_boss_candidate.mjs`. It asserts the installed client skill costs, targets, base coefficients, school IDs and Jade parameters. At level-one base coefficients, one Long Hate contributes `0.25 × Helot BattleAtkForce` per enemy before Bleeding and combat modifiers. Two *conditional* plays contribute `0.50 × Helot BattleAtkForce` per enemy; without healing or other HP changes, two 10%-of-current-HP payments leave `0.9² = 81%` of starting HP. One base Mortal Blast contributes two hits of `0.15 × Mouchette BattleAtkForce`, or `0.30 × Mouchette BattleAtkForce` per enemy. These expressions cannot be added into a numeric total without each Awakener's resolved stats and target defenses. Jade's discard and draw copies do not guarantee a second play on the same turn.

The script intentionally reports `CARD_RULES_ONLY`. It does not resolve the hand, energy and Posse schedule, Bleeding ticks, enemy attacks, final damage, healing, or a win. The audited SS fight initializes only the Sage; it does not test this mob-clear line or the tier-7 encounter. The next useful test is a frozen E0 build and explicit starting hand against a recorded multi-enemy stage, followed by an independent Sage replay. Do not spend account resources or enter a battle on the basis of this document alone.

## Retrospective mobbing check — candidate not validated

Run `python tools/audit_mob_clear_candidate.py` to reproduce the sanitized [replay audit](../research/evidence/season71-mob-clear-candidate-audit.json). In a historical stage-81781 replay, a **level-90, potency-15, skill-6 Mouchette** used Mortal Blast against three enemies. One action recorded nine hits and 48,351 total damage: 20,721 to the boss and 13,815 to each of two smaller enemies. Those smaller enemies had 146,992 maximum HP and were already at 76,821 and 95,735 HP; they remained at **63,006 and 81,920 HP** after the card. A second observed three-target Blast also left both smaller enemies alive. This validates multi-target hit routing in that historical battle but **fails a one-card mob-clear test even for this invested Mouchette**. It cannot be extrapolated as an E0 damage result or a Season 71 enemy-HP estimate.

The sampled Helot Long Hate replay actions number 4, 5 and 2 across three other historical records. Every sampled action had only one live target, so they establish **no gameplay validation of Helot against a pack**. Its `AllEnemy` selector and 0.25 × BattleAtkForce coefficient are source rules, not measured multi-enemy throughput. There is no replay of this proposed four-character team. The previously described E0 team should therefore be treated as a testable hypothesis, **not a validated budget recommendation**. A valid mobbing check needs a recorded multi-enemy fight with Helot's card, actual E-levels and builds, a full action/energy schedule, and enemy HP after Bleeding and counterattacks.
